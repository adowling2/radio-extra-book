# Round 7 Generalization — Front Matter and Chapters 1–11

## Scope

This contextual Phase 5 pass reviewed the front matter and Chapters 1–11 after the
detailed markup work. It applies the confirmed rules: direct actors and causal links,
local definitions, removal of repeated setup or conclusion, and punctuation chosen for
logic rather than emphasis. It does not mechanically replace em dashes, change a

  the Bode asymptotes.
- **Chapter 5:** Defined the plant/forward-path terminology with direct punctuation and
  named feedback, rather than an abstract trade, as the actor that exchanges gain for
  speed.
- **Chapter 6:** Tightened the stable all-pole factoring transition and the cascade
  explanation without weakening the existing stable/all-pole, delay, and
  linear-phase qualifications.
- **Chapter 10:** Made energy the explicit fifth circuit view, stated the state-count
  bridge before the circuit map, and connected the table to the reusable
  first-/second-order results.

### Punctuation and repetition

- Replaced selected rhetorical em-dash joins with periods, colons, commas, or
  semicolons where those marks make the relation between claims explicit.
- Kept structural part labels, compound terms, mathematical signs, ranges, and
  parenthetical technical qualifiers intact.
- Removed only repeated framing where the surrounding equation, model, or existing
  figure already carries the explanation.
